About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the University of Toronto and a member of the Krembil Family Epigenetics Laboratory at the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH). I am advised by Adam Stinchcombe and Arturas Petronis.

My research uses techniques from applied mathematics to advance our understanding of chronobiology. I am particularly interested in applications of dynamical systems theory, circular statistics, and optimal experimental design.

For more information, see my publications and CV.
