Research

Research interests

My research interests include:

  • Mechanistic models of circadian and neural systems
  • Circular statistics
  • Optimal experimental design
  • Scientific computing
  • Analysis of dynamical systems

Mathematical biology

I study mathematical questions arising in chronobiology and neuroscience. In particular, I am interested in how mechanistic models of biological oscillators can connect processes operating on different timescales to the rhythms observed in experiments.

My recent work considers how the timing of measurements affects the statistical power of rhythm-detection experiments, and how mathematical optimization can improve experimental designs when the period of a rhythm is unknown.

Mathematical physics

I am also interested in mathematical structures that arise in quantum information and mathematical physics. Earlier work explored connections between graph theory, group representations, and nonlocal games.

One example is the relationship between graph minors and the existence of perfect quantum strategies for graph-incidence games.
